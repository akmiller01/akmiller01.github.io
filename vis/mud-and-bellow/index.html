Why hippos?

Because lions can wait. Hippos can't.

  • Closer to whales than to pigs. Hippos' nearest living relatives are cetaceans. Their ancestors left the water, regretted it, and came right back.
  • They make their own sunscreen. That famous pink "blood sweat" is a red secretion that works as SPF and an antibacterial. It's not blood. Probably.
  • The 180° grin. They can open their mouths wide enough to fit a small car's wheel. The smile is for show. The bite is not.
  • Africa's most dangerous big mammal. Hippos cause more human fatalities on the continent than lions, leopards or crocodiles. We keep our boats at a respectful distance.
